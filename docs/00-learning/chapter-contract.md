# Chapter Contract

A publication chapter is incomplete unless it contains:

1. **One-sentence purpose** — what problem the system solves.
2. **Real-world visual** — authoritative source or real lab screenshot.
3. **Memory model** — 3–7 items maximum.
4. **Worked example** — complete start-to-finish example.
5. **Lab walkthrough** — commands and expected observations.
6. **Protocol/message view** — when a network/serial interface is involved.
7. **Failure contrast** — how normal and abnormal differ.
8. **Evidence to save** — exact artifacts.
9. **60-second explain-back** — test whether the reader can teach it.
10. **Limitations/source** — what we simplified and where the real claim comes from.

No chapter may use a large table as the primary explanation.
